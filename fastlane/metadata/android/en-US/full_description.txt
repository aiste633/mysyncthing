<p>MySyncthing runs <a href="https://syncthing.net">Syncthing</a> on Android 15 and 16. Syncthing keeps folders in sync between your devices, directly and encrypted, without a cloud service in between.</p>
<p><b>Home-screen widget:</b> one slim row shows whether everything is up to date, syncing (with progress), paused or needs your attention, and which devices are connected. Its button pauses and resumes syncing.</p>
<ul>
<li>Pair devices by QR code or device ID, and accept folders shared with you, with a suggested location, folder type and file versioning</li>
<li>Native Material 3 screens for folder and device settings, with Material You colours and dark mode</li>
<li>Notifications for new devices and shared folders, with Review and Ignore</li>
<li>Runs as a foreground service all day, without the 6-hour limit of data-sync services, and starts at boot</li>
<li>Run conditions: metered Wi-Fi, mobile data and roaming each on or off, only while charging or only on battery, pause in battery saver, only at set times of the week</li>
<li>SOCKS5 or HTTP proxy, such as Tor with Orbot</li>
<li>Easy switch from Syncthing-Fork: import its backup, encrypted ones too; export your own setup, optionally AES-256 encrypted</li>
<li>Syncthing's own web interface is built in for advanced settings</li>
<li>In English, Italian, Spanish, German and French, in the phone's language or one chosen for the app alone; dates, times and numbers as the phone's region writes them</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Private:</b> no tracking, advertising or analytics, and no account. Syncthing's anonymous usage reporting is off unless you agree to it. Cloud backup is off, so the device keys never go to a cloud; a phone-to-phone transfer to a new phone carries them, so stop Syncthing on the old phone afterwards. <a href="https://github.com/aiste633/mysyncthing/blob/main/docs/privacy.md">What the app keeps and what leaves the phone</a>.</p>
<p><b>Light on battery and flash:</b> when idle, the app writes about 0.02–0.14 MB an hour to flash, and it keeps no log files.</p>
<p><b>Hardened and tested:</b> every input checked against the core's own rules with property tests, Go fuzzing, a security audit, stress tests with 50,000 files and full storage, a 10-hour memory soak, and interruption tests. Reproducible builds and a full software bill of materials let anyone check what the APK contains.</p>
<p><b>Vibecoded:</b> an AI coding assistant (Claude) wrote nearly all of the code, directed and tested by one person. No guarantees if something breaks or goes wrong, as the licence says: keep backups of anything that matters.</p>
<p>MySyncthing is free software (AGPL-3.0-or-later), and so is everything it contains. The official Syncthing core (v2) runs inside the app, with a few small documented fixes. Reaching folders anywhere on shared storage needs the "All files access" permission.</p>
