<p>MySyncthing runs <a href="https://syncthing.net">Syncthing</a> on Android 15 and 16. Syncthing keeps folders in sync between your devices, directly and encrypted, without a cloud service in between.</p>
<p><b>Home-screen widget:</b> one slim row shows whether everything is up to date, syncing (with progress), paused or needs your attention, and which devices are connected. Its button pauses and resumes syncing.</p>
<ul>
<li>Pair devices by QR code or device ID, and accept folders shared with you, with a suggested location, folder type and file versioning</li>
<li>Folder and device settings, with Material You colours and dark mode</li>
<li>Notifications for new devices and shared folders, with Review and Ignore</li>
<li>Runs as a foreground service and starts at boot</li>
<li>Run conditions: metered Wi-Fi, mobile data and roaming each on or off, only while charging or only on battery, pause in battery saver, only at set times of the week</li>
<li>SOCKS5 or HTTP proxy, such as Tor with Orbot</li>
<li>Backup export, optionally AES-256 encrypted, and import, also from Syncthing-Fork</li>
<li>Syncthing's own web interface is built in for advanced settings</li>
<li>In English and Italian, in the phone's language or one chosen for the app alone; dates, times and numbers as the phone's region writes them</li>
</ul>
<p>The Syncthing core (v2) runs inside the app. Reaching folders anywhere on shared storage needs the "All files access" permission.</p>
<p>No ads, no tracking. Privacy: <a href="https://github.com/aiste633/mysyncthing/blob/main/docs/privacy.md">what the app keeps and what leaves the phone</a>.</p>
