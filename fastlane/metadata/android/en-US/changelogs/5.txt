Pause single devices from their card; a device you paused stays paused. Devices can have their own sync windows within the global ones. The Devices tab has the switches for all devices and shows when each device was last seen. No device connected is grey for 30 min, then red. Every setting shows its default value, and address fields have a "?" with the formats. The widget and notifications keep the app's language. Fixes from a long random-input test.
